Tools

Pedigree Analyzer Draw a family, record who is affected, and compare how well each inheritance model explains the observations. Pedigree Solver Choose the inheritance model and solve for unknown individuals using the whole family structure. Punnett Squares Cross up to four independent loci and see every offspring genotype with its exact probability. Gene Analyzer Translate DNA or RNA with the standard genetic code and inspect predicted ORFs across six frames. Nucleotide Flipper Generate complementary, reverse-complementary, or transcribed sequences, including IUPAC codes.
