Paste a sequence on the left. Complement rules: DNA A↔T and C↔G; RNA A↔U and C↔G; a DNA template read into RNA maps A→U, T→A, C→G, G→C. IUPAC codes are complemented too (R↔Y, K↔M, B↔V, D↔H, S, W, and N stay self-complementary).

Supported conversions and notation

Complement

  • DNA → DNA: A↔T, C↔G
  • RNA → RNA: A↔U, C↔G
  • DNA template → RNA: A→U, T→A, C→G, G→C
  • RNA template → DNA: A→T, U→A, C→G, G→C

Orientation

  • Complement keeps the 5′→3′ order and replaces each base.
  • Reverse complement complements, then reverses the order.
  • Coding strand → mRNA is transcription, not complementation: T becomes U and no bases are paired.

IUPAC ambiguity codes

R↔Y, K↔M, B↔V, D↔H; S, W, and N are self-complementary. Uppercase and lowercase input are accepted; output is uppercase.
